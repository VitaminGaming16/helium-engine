Character sheet is a list of properties each entity has. There are different sheets including [[#Player Character Sheet]] (PCS) and [[#Simplified NPC Sheet]]. DM can use PCS for NPCs, its just not as convenient as SNPCS for the sheer amount of different NPCs across campaign.


# Player Character Sheet
Player character sheet is full version of Character Sheet and includes all parameters that could benefit roleplaying and character building. There are two categories of parameters: First one is verbose parameters: name, appearance, etc; Second one is statblock, these are just skills of character.

## Verbose parameters
- Name
- Age
- Race
- Behaviour
- Class
- Hobby
- Appearance (includes description of perceived: age, height, body type, eyes color, skin color/scars, hair color/style)
- Biography
## Statblock
Statblock is separated into 4 categories called Attributes and those Attributes branch off into 12 Skills. Each Attribute scales from 1 to 12 and each Skill scales from 1 to 6. Attribute values are distributed at the character building step by player themselves using a pool of points while Skills are defined by `Attribute/3` (rounded down) and can be increased by bonuses from race/class/behaviour/hobby/external tags (weapons, effects).

### Attributes and their skills

| **Attribute**      | **Skill 1** |      **Skill 2**      | **Skill 3**  |
| ------------------ | :---------: | :-------------------: | :----------: |
| **[[#Body]]**      |  Strength   |      Durability       |    Health    |
| **[[#Mind]]**      | Temperance  |        Memory         | Intelligence |
| **[[#Speed]]**     |   Agility   | Hand-Eye Coordination |  Dexterity   |
| **[[#Affection]]** | Persuasion  |      Perception       |     Look     |

### Skills
#### Body
- Strength (STR) - ability to move heavy objects and apply physical power
- Durability (DUR) - durability of character's body
- Health (HLT) - immunity to diseases and regeneration of wounds
#### Mind
- Temperance (TMP) - mental durability against stress
- Memory (MEM) - ability to memorize information and recall previous events
- Intelligence (INT) - information analysis and processing
#### Speed
- Agility (AGL) - movement speed, both horizontal and vertical
- Hand-Eye Coordination (HEC) - reaction speed and precision
- Dexterity (DEX) - local speed of body and dodge speed
#### Affection
- Persuasion (PRS) - persuading others and deception
- Perception (PRC) - perception of nonverbal and verbal communication cues from other people
- Look (LOK) - overall perceived by others appearance of character including visual, smell, speech, behaviour

# Simplified NPC Sheet
Simplified NPC Sheet is minimalistic light sheet for DM to put on any NPC. The reason this exists is because its not practical to put [[#Player Character Sheet]] on every NPC including random monsters, citizens, peasants, etc. It still contains Verbose parameters and Statblock, but they have far less parameters overall. Its not mandatory to use SNPCS for all NPCs but rather those who shouldnt be worked on too much, so bosses may still have full [[#Player Character Sheet]]. Also note that if you need to include parameter that's not mentioned in SNPCS, you may add it from full PCS, its just minimal amount of parameters, not strict set of them.

## Verbose parameters
- Name
- Race
- Class


## Statblock
Since they lack hobby and behaviour, they also dont have skills! If you need to roll for skill check, then bonus from skill is gonna be `ATTRIBUTE/3`
