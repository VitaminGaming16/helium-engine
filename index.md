# Helium Engine
Helium engine is lightweight TTRPG system engine. Word "engine" means that its just foundation, you cant play engine itself but rather build your own system on top of it. It serves as DnD alternative for more opportunities for dynamic fights, better social interactions and most importantly less homebrew (changing already existing mechanics) but actual system building. Note that while Helium Engine aims to grant all these pros to systems built on top of it, it still depends on exact system implementation, DM and players.

# FAQ
### Why does this system engine exist?
My first TTRPG campaigns were DnD campaigns but with so many homebrew that it was hard to call it DnD. It was used for all sorts of settings, such as fantasy, post-modern world, etc. I decided to make my own system for one specific campaign, but then after I gathered a lot of ideas for many different systems and they all shared the same ideas that were blatant homebrew that was changing half of DnD mechanics, I decided to make my own engine and build systems on top of it.

### Who is this engine for?
Im specifically building this engine for virtual TTRPG like Foundry VTT, Owlbear etc. Inventory, hp and other values arent included in [[Character sheet]] but rather handled by external apps for convenience.
This engine is built for being lightweight while supporting heavy additions and changes. Change engine into something absolutely different or keep it simple, its up to you.
